        Pomodoro Timer

        DESCRIPTION

        A structured focus timer built to sustain deep, distraction-free work sessions paired with scheduled recovery breaks.

        DURATION & CYCLE

        25 minutes of Focus followed by a 5-minute Short Break, repeated 4 times (4 rounds), ending with a 15-minute Long Break.

        SESSION RULE

        Unpausable — once started, the timer runs continuously through the entire cycle and cannot be paused.
